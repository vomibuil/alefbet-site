Hanukkah in Hebrew

Hanukkah is the eight-day Festival of Lights (חג האורים): light the hanukkiah, eat sufganiyot and latkes, spin the dreidel with the letters נ ג ה פ — “A great miracle happened here”. In 2026 Hanukkah runs from the evening of December 4 to December 12.
