How to say “certainly” in Hebrew

adverbA1
בְּווַדַּאי
certainly, definitely, of course
bevadai

Examples

בְּווַדַּאי אָנִי בָּא לַמִּסְעָדָה בְּשָׁעוּר שׁמוֹנֶה.

I will definitely come to the restaurant at eight o'clock.
