How to say “As opposed to” in Hebrew

prepositionA1
לְעוּמַת
As opposed to; compared to; compared with; versus; by contrast
lumat
Root · שורש ע-מ-ת

Examples

לְעוּמַת הַחוֹרֶף, הַקַּיִץ מְאוֹד חָם.

Compared to winter, summer is very hot.
