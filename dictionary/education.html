How to say “education” in Hebrew

nounA1
חִינּוּךְ
education, upbringing
hinuh
Root · שורש ח-נ-כ

Examples

חִינּוּךְ טוֹב הוּא הַבָּסִיס לְחַייִּים מוּצְלָחִים.

The education system in Israel is very developed.
