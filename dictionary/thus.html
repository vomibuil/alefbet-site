How to say “thus” in Hebrew

adverbA1
כָּכָה
thus, so, this way, in this manner; "because", for no reason, that's just the way it is (coll.)
kaha

Examples

כָּכָה הוּא עָשָׂה וְנִשְׁמַר מִין־הַטָּעוּת.

That's how he acted and avoided the mistake.
